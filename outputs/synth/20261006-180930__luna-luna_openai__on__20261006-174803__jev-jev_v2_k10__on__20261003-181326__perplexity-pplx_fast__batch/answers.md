Bezlotoxumab (Zinplava; CDB1) is the named anti-TcdB monoclonal-antibody comparator. It binds two epitopes in the TcdB CROPS near the hinge, whereas AZD5148 binds the TcdB glucosyltransferase domain (GTD); the reported epitope conservation was 82.47% for bezlotoxumab and 99.58% for AZD5148 across 9,134 global isolates. Bezlotoxumab was approved as a single intravenous infusion of 10 mg/kg in the USA and European Union to prevent recurrent CDI in high-risk people receiving antibacterial treatment; its production was discontinued in January 2025. [E1][E2][E3][E4][E5] AZD5148, formerly PA41, is an anti-TcdB humanized monoclonal antibody that blocks delivery of the GTD enzymatic cargo into host-cell cytosol. It has a YTE Fc substitution (M252Y/S254T/T256E), and the stated strategy targets the GTD independently of the target cell’s receptor. [E1][E3][E4][E5] In vitro, AZD5148 neutralized TcdB1 and TcdB2 with EC50 values 1,000- to 14,000-fold lower than bezlotoxumab and neutralized eight recent circulating TcdB ribotypes; in mouse CDI models it protected against weight loss and mortality at significantly lower doses than bezlotoxumab. It also conferred protection in a gnotobiotic piglet CDI model and mouse models involving different TcdB ribotypes. Adding anti-TcdA mAb PA50 provided no additional protective benefit in the reported mouse study. [E3][E4][E5] AstraZeneca’s H1/Q2 2026 appendix lists AZD5148’s Phase IIb PRISM study, NCT07285213, in 230 adults with a qualifying CDI episode: randomized, double-blind and placebo-controlled, with AZD5148 or placebo, primary endpoint efficacy, secondary endpoints safety and pharmacokinetics, first-patient-clinical-dose in Q4 2025, and data anticipated after 2027. [E7] The appendix also lists Phase I NCT06469151 in 84 healthy volunteers as randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled dose escalation: cohort 1 received dose 1 intramuscularly; cohorts 2a and 2b dose 2 intramuscularly, with cohort 2b Chinese participants; cohort 3 dose 2 intravenously; cohorts 4a and 4b dose 3 intravenously, with cohort 4b Chinese participants; and cohort 5 dose 4 intravenously. Its primary endpoint was safety, secondary endpoint pharmacokinetic parameters, first patient in Q2 2024, last patient in Q4 2024, and data readout Q1 2026. [E7] A 2025 profile lists three active clinical trials: D8820C00005, Phase I, active but not recruiting, for Clostridium infections, with primary completion 2025-12-29 and a 69% probability of success; jRCT2071240071, Phase I, not yet recruiting, in healthy volunteers, with primary completion 2026-01-12; and D8820C00002, Phase I, active but not recruiting, in healthy volunteers, with primary completion 2025-11-07 and a 69% probability of success.
