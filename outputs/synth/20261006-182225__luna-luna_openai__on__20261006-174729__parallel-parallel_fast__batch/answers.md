At Week 48 in the overall Crohn’s disease population, high-dose JNJ-4804 achieved clinical remission in 50.8% and endoscopic response in 38.1%, versus 25.4% and 19.8%, respectively, with golimumab; the corresponding guselkumab rates were 42.5% and 33.9%. In the DUET-CD population with disease refractory to at least two systemic therapy classes, the reported treatment differences for high-dose JNJ-4804 versus golimumab were 25.7 percentage points for clinical remission and 18.5 points for endoscopic response (nominal P<.001 for both).
